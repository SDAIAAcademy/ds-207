# Student Housing: the room priority rule

**Unit.** Student Housing Office, Al-Qimam University (fictional). 4,000 synthetic records in `records.csv`.

**The rule.** A student gets priority for a campus room when the recorded home distance is above 150 km or the family income band is the lowest. The flag is the column `priority_given`; the outcome the rule is meant
to find is the column `needed_room`.

**The sponsor's request.** The director of student housing: "We have 300 complaints about room allocation. Prove the priority rule is fair."

**The partner's request.** In `partner_request.json`.

**Known about the data.** Ask the trainer only after your own group comparison: something in the
way one field is recorded differs for one group.
