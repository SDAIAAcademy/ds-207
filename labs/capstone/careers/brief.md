# Career Centre: the internship shortlist rule

**Unit.** Career Centre, Al-Qimam University (fictional). 4,000 synthetic records in `records.csv`.

**The rule.** A student is shortlisted for an internship interview when the grade average is at least 3.75 or the student attended at least three career workshops. The flag is the column `shortlisted`; the outcome the rule is meant
to find is the column `ready_for_internship`.

**The sponsor's request.** The dean of business: "Send me the names of the students who were not shortlisted, with the reason, so their departments can push them."

**The partner's request.** In `partner_request.json`.

**Known about the data.** Ask the trainer only after your own group comparison: something in the
way one field is recorded differs for one group.
