# Scholarship Office: the award review rule

**Unit.** Scholarship Office, Al-Qimam University (fictional). 4,000 synthetic records in `records.csv`.

**The rule.** An award is flagged for review when the term grade average is below 3.0 or the recorded study load is below 12 credit hours. The flag is the column `flagged_for_review`; the outcome the rule is meant
to find is the column `award_withdrawn`.

**The sponsor's request.** The vice-rector for student affairs: "Tell me which scholarship students do not deserve their award, so we can free the money before next term."

**The partner's request.** In `partner_request.json`.

**Known about the data.** Ask the trainer only after your own group comparison: something in the
way one field is recorded differs for one group.
