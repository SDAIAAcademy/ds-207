Every decision has a reason and an owner. "Refused" or "suppressed" without the rule, the harm and the approver is a guess, not a decision.

Full marks, one example each. Legal: the decision names the basis, the level, the form of release, the conditions and who signs. Tables: no cell under 10, complementary suppression done, the differencing check shown. Comparison: intervals, two candidate bias types, the check that separates them. Documentation: a peer could answer the audit questions. Scope note: an answerable question and what the analysis must not be used for.

Format

Eight minutes for each defence

1 · The pack · 4 minutes

The engagement, the sharing decision, one release table, the largest gap between groups and its likely cause.

2 · The role-play · 2 minutes

A peer plays the sponsor of your brief and presses. You rescope the request. No AI assistant, no notes on screen.

3 · One fact changes · 2 minutes

The assessor changes one fact of the partner's request. You revise the sharing decision aloud.

Peer feedback. After each defence, everyone writes one strength, one question and one suggestion.

Facts to change, one per defence: the partner will store the data outside the Kingdom; the partner now asks for row-level data instead of tables; the partner becomes a government entity, or stops being one; the purpose changes to marketing; a field with health data is added. Score the role-play with the five behaviours of Lab 3.3.
